Be the Answer AI Recommends.

AEO SEO ensures your brand is cited, referenced, and recommended by ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

The Next Frontier of Search Is Here

Millions of searches are now answered directly by AI systems without the user ever visiting a website. AEO SEO is the discipline of optimising your brand so these AI systems choose to reference, quote, and recommend you. Brands investing in AEO now are gaining a substantial first-mover advantage.

What AEO SEO Includes

  • AI search landscape audit: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Overviews

  • Knowledge graph and entity-based SEO
